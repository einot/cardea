Cardea

Your lock box, shared safely

Cardea opens your Bluetooth lock box with one slide, and shares it with the people you choose. There are no accounts and no manufacturer cloud: the phone talks to the lock directly, and everything shared goes through iCloud, end-to-end encrypted.
